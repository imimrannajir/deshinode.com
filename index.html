FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy hosting without a domain?

Yes. You can buy hosting on its own and connect or transfer a domain later.

Can you migrate my existing website for free?

Yes. All shared hosting plans from Spark to Surge include free, automatic website migration.

Is SSL included?

Shared hosting plans include unlimited free SSL. Standalone SSL certificates are also available.

What is the difference between BDIX and USA VPS?

BDIX VPS servers are in Dhaka and provide low-latency connectivity within Bangladesh. USA VPS servers are in a US data center and suit audiences outside Bangladesh.
