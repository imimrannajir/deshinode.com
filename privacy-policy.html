Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeshiNode collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you visit deshinode.com, order our services or contact our support team.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways.

  • Information you give us: name, email address, phone number, billing address, company name, domain registrant details and support messages. Payment details are processed by our payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and referring pages. This is recorded by our web server and by analytics tools.

2. How we use your information

  • Providing, activating and managing your services.
  • Processing payments, invoices and renewals.
  • Registering and managing domains, which requires WHOIS registrant details under registry rules.
  • Responding to support requests and enquiries.
  • Protecting our network and customers from fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Sending service notices, and marketing messages where you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in to the client area, remember settings such as currency, and measure how the website is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the website may not work properly without them.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary:

  • With domain registries and registrars, to register or transfer your domain.
  • With payment processors, to take payments.
  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as hosting infrastructure, email delivery and certificate authorities, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Where required by law, a court order or a lawful request from authorities.
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of DeshiNode, our customers or the public.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and security monitoring. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that we can meet accounting, tax and legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information by opening a support ticket. We may need to keep some information where the law requires it, or to complete a service you have ordered. You can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any message.

8. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, so please read their policies.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business and adult customers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact sales@deshinode.com or +880 176 598 7173. Address: Khilbarirtek, Gulshan, Badda, Dhaka, Bangladesh.
